The green slime is a palette variant of **rvros' [Animated Pixel Slime](https://rvros.itch.io/pixel-art-animated-slime)**, released under CC0. Dmap replaces five blue shades with greens while preserving the original animation, outline, mouth and highlights.

Dmap preparations: ordered animation frames, cropped/padded sheets, exact mirrors for opposite directions, compact sprite atlases, reduced spell dimensions and crisp alpha edges. The guardian has native side-view poses, mirrored for left/right; up/down reuse its side view. Spell art originated as vectors; its raster frames are reduced for the game. No promotional preview images are used as source sprites.

[Provenance and file hashes](provenance.json) record source archives, selected production files, transformations, dimensions and frame coordinates. Rebuild with `scripts/import-magic-demo.py` and the pinned source archives. These files support this playable demo; original archives and unused pack contents are not included here.
