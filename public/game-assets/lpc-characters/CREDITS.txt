Modifications and rendering
---------------------------
All 36 imported animation PNGs are unchanged original files. Dmap places
body, head, pants, shirt, boots and hair in six synchronized Phaser layers.
The game and NPCs share these appearances and the game-owned texture cache.
Each portrait.png is a derived 40x40 transparent image: Canvas 2D composition
of its six front idle frame-zero layers, cropped from the source sheets at
x=12, y=136, width=40, height=40, with no recoloring or resampling.
Portraits retain the same credits and OGA-BY 3.0 license.

Frame dimensions: 64x64. Feet anchor: x=32, y=62 native pixels.
Direction rows: up, left, down, right. Idle: 3 frames per direction.
Walk and dedicated run: 8 frames per direction, all six layers present.
Body/head silhouette: 44–46px for idle/walk, 43–50px during the run cycle.
Collision footprint: 18x12 pixels around the feet; the 64x64 transparent
frame canvas is not the collision body.
