Current wardrobe refresh (2026-09-09)
------------------------------------
Twenty selectable looks reuse original LPC body, head, trousers and boots,
with eight newly imported buttoned long-sleeve shirt palettes and ten original
hair style/color combinations. Female looks now use high-neck buttoned shirts
in forest, wine, indigo or charcoal. The earlier yellow shirt and female plate
silhouette are no longer selected by the live catalog. Saved appearance IDs
remain valid; some of their clothing and hairstyles have been refreshed.

resampling or baked frame substitutions. Existing runtime skin and trouser
tints remain where configured; light hair uses the artist's actual blonde,
platinum, silver, orange and white palettes. The portraits and playable rigs
share these source layers and runtime tints.

Modifications and rendering
---------------------------
The 36 Rowan/Ash animation PNGs are unchanged original files. Dmap places
body, head, pants, shirt, boots and hair in six synchronized Phaser layers.
The game and NPCs share these appearances and the game-owned texture cache.
Each of the four portrait.png files is a derived 40x40 transparent image: Canvas 2D composition
of its six front idle frame-zero layers, cropped from the source sheets at
x=12, y=136, width=40, height=40, with no recoloring or resampling.
Portraits retain the same credits and OGA-BY 3.0 license.

Frame dimensions: 64x64. Feet anchor: x=32, y=62 native pixels.
Direction rows: up, left, down, right. Idle: 3 frames per direction.
Walk and dedicated run: 8 frames per direction, all six layers present.
Body/head silhouette: 44–46px for idle/walk, 43–50px during the run cycle.
Collision footprint: 18x12 pixels around the feet; the 64x64 transparent
frame canvas is not the collision body.
