# Forest waygates

The vine-covered arch reuses the project's licensed CraftPix Free Ruined Temple asset,
unchanged at `/game-assets/ruined-temple/arch.png`. See that directory's CREDITS.md,
provenance.json and license for the original source and terms.

Source: https://craftpix.net/freebies/free-ruined-temple-top-down-location-pixel-art/

`circle.svg` and the Hearthstone inventory symbol are original Dmap vector geometry.
Recall reuses Luke.RUSTLTD's CC0 summoning-circle vector, already credited with its
original source and license in `/game-assets/ritual-sigils/CREDITS.md`.
Animated particles reuse the unmodified CC0 Kenney sparkle PNGs already credited in
`/game-assets/navigation-sparkles/CREDITS.md`.
Tinting, sizing and motion are runtime presentation. No source bitmap was edited.
The shrine composition uses muted stone, moss and warm gold to match the forest.
