# Combat and consumable effects

- **Viktor Hahn (Unnamed)** — [Pixelated Attack/Hit Animations](https://opengameart.org/content/pixelated-attackhit-animations), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). `slash.png` is `hit07.png`, `swing.png` is `swing03.png`, `impact.png` is `splash01.png`.
- **CodeManu / David Masia** — [Free Pixel Effects Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/free-pixel-effects-pack), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). `healing.png` is `17_felspell_spritesheet.png`, `battle.png` is `6_flamelash_spritesheet.png`, `swiftstep.png` is `4_casting_spritesheet.png`.

All six PNGs are the unmodified original sheets, hosted locally. Frame selection, timing, scaling, directional rotation and tints happen at runtime. Original names, source links and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `provenance.json`.

Traveler eating/pouring, recoil, defeat poses and particles are runtime choreography by Dmap over the existing credited LPC character rig. No new character-sheet pixels are distributed.
