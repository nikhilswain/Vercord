Original 512x256 cat and dog PNGs are distributed unchanged.
Dmap selects authored 32x32 frames and coat palettes at runtime.
The four walking directions use atlas rows right, up, down, left;
columns 0-2 in each four-column coat block. No pixels are repainted.

Per-file original URLs, hashes and dimensions: manifest.json
Reproduce with: node scripts/import-lpc-animals.mjs
