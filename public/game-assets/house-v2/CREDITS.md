# House interiors — LPC Revised

All PNGs are unmodified originals from [Eliza Wyatt’s LPC Revised collection](https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC), pinned to commit `f07f7f5892e67c932c68f70bb04472f2c64e46bc`. See `manifest.json` for exact source URLs and SHA-256 digests. Runtime crops, layering, sizing, and scene layouts are defined in `src/domain/world/content/house-v2/`.

Used under **OGA-BY 3.0**. The complete license is in `OGA-BY-3.0.txt`. The pinned source directories’ full per-asset attribution declarations are retained in `credits-*.txt` alongside these images.

Artists: **Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm), Eliza Wyatt (DeathsDarling), BlueCarrot16, and Hyptosis**, with per-asset authorship and original-source links in those credit files. These files also include notices for unused assets in the original directories; no ownership of that art is claimed by Dmap.

- Fireplace, animated fire, clock, cauldron, desks, furniture, planters, workbench: `credits-furniture.txt` and `credits-small-items.txt`.
- Beds: `credits-beds.txt`.
- Chairs: `credits-seating.txt`.
- Spinning wheel: `credits-weaving.txt`.
- Floorboards: `credits-floor.txt`.
- Tea things, bottles, candles, paper: `credits-small-items.txt`.

The animated hearth originates from [LPC Fireplace by Sharm](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-fireplace). The spinning wheel originates from [LPC Tailor](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-tailor). This distribution uses the pinned LPC Revised versions and their OGA-BY declarations.

Existing original Dmap wall, rug, counter, bookshelf, and doorway art is reused from `../house-v1/furniture.svg`; that source is unchanged so older saved rooms remain readable.
